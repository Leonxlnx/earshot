# Credits: the fitted sounds

The bank's fitted sounds (`earshot/forge/bank/fit.json`, version `fit1`) are recipes measured from these
recordings on [Freesound](https://freesound.org). Every one is released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain), which asks for no attribution; the
recordists are credited here anyway, with thanks. A fit stores the structure of a sound (modes, partials, band
envelopes), not its samples: no recording ships with earshot.
