## What was tuned on what

- **Detector thresholds** (salient hit: >= 6 dB jump of the 100 ms level within 80 ms, landing within 8 LU of integrated, one hit per run, timed at the attack; visual event: frame-difference peak >= 4x the median of the surrounding second or >= 0.15, now earshot.video.diff_events; stop-down: >= 10 dB dip for 0.15-1.5 s) were chosen by looking at plots of all 9 films (7 refs + 2 Clawd), at the Clawd mix-report placements (hits) and at ffmpeg scene cuts (visual events). The Clawd real soundtrack is therefore partly tuning data for the hit detector.
- **Music threshold** (AudioSet Music >= 0.3 per 5 s window) was set after looking at the per-window probabilities of all 9 films (music under voice or SFX scored 0.4-0.7, one sound-design stretch 0.1-0.15).
- **Changed after the first leave-one-out run** (first build; not a blind test of these choices): true peak went from critical to major and is judged against 0 dBTP for decoded lossy files (the -1 dBTP master rule failed all 7 AAC-decoded references); a critical rule missed by <= 2 tolerances counts as major; the metric max_gap_s was added; the music_frac tolerance went from 0.1 to 0.05 after an SFX-only stem (music in 42 % of windows) came out as only a near miss.
- **Changed after the independent review, before the known-bad set below was first run:** p_real uses the realness model's own thresholds (ok at its 'real or pro-produced' threshold, critical below 0.5, minor in between; no near-miss downgrade); a density range that would reach 0 keeps a floor at half the sparsest reference (salient hits >= 0.05 /s); a mono mix (identical channels) counts as -40 dB stereo width instead of n.a.; files under 10 s get no loudness-shape rows; brightness_hz replaces the power-weighted centroid (info only). The known-bad expectations were written into run_eval.py before its first run; nothing was changed after seeing its results.
- **Not adjusted after seeing results:** the other tolerances, the weights of the other metrics and the verdict thresholds (90 / 75 / 50 %).
- **Ranges** are data: min-max of the reference films widened by each metric's tolerance. Loudness and true peak use web-delivery practice (-16..-9 LUFS; <= -1 dBTP for a master, <= 0 dBTP for decoded AAC / MP3) instead, so references can fail them.

## The Clawd film: real soundtrack vs the retired synthesized soundtrack

Reading: the scorecard rates the real soundtrack close (90%) and the synthesized one needs work (80%). The structural metrics separate them only a little: both are loud enough, continuous and on the picture; the synth version fades away at the end, is sparser and has a long fade, while the real one starts late (a 1 s near-silent cold open) and has one loudness peak that sticks out. The difference a listener hears most - synthesized timbre - is not structural; it is what p_real measures: real 0.94 vs synth 0.33 (earshot.realness, verdict thresholds lo 0.4 / hi 0.77 at this run); below 0.5 the p_real row is critical, which is what moves the synth version's verdict. Caveat: the realness model (another module) was trained partly on these 7 launch films, on the Clawd project's real asset library and on the retired synthesized stems (eval/realness/), so p_real on the Clawd films is not an independent test.

## Hit / onset timing vs ground truth (tuning data: the Clawd real soundtrack)

The mix report lists 264 sample placements (every key tick, pop, whoosh, hit, music motif note ...). earshot found 16 salient hits and 149 onsets in the mixed film audio; 11 of the 16 hits sit within 2 ms of a placement (median distance 1.0 ms).

| tolerance | hits on a placement (chance) | onsets on a placement | placements with an onset |
|---|---|---|---|
| +-20 ms | 75% (10%) | 61% | 62% |
| +-50 ms | 88% (25%) | 75% | 78% |
| +-100 ms | 88% (39%) | 79% | 83% |

Chance = the same hits shifted off the placements by fixed offsets. Hit and onset times are moved to the steepest rise of the 2 ms level (attack), which is how a mixer aligns a sample.
